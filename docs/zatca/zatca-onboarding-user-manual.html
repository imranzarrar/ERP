Everything they show matches the running application at the time this manual was written.

Before you start

Who can do this. Only a Company Admin or Super Admin can see and use this screen. It cannot be delegated to a staff role, even a very trusted one — there is no permission toggle for it by design, because it touches the company's cryptographic signing keys.

One important thing to un-learn first: the Company Profile tab has its own "VAT Number" field. That field is cosmetic only — it prints on invoice templates and shows on the company card. It is completely separate from ZATCA onboarding and is never sent to ZATCA. The real tax identity used for ZATCA lives inside the ZATCA wizard itself, entered independently for each of the three environments.

The single idea this whole manual is built on

Sandbox, Simulation, and Production are three completely independent onboarding processes for the same company. Each has its own identity, its own cryptographic key, its own certificates, and its own "done" state. Finishing Sandbox does not advance Simulation or Production even slightly — you will walk through the exact same 5 steps three separate times, once per environment, on purpose.

The three environments, in the order almost every company should actually use them:

  1. Sandbox — a free ZATCA test environment. It uses ZATCA's own published test company identity, not yours, and every field pre-fills automatically. This is where you learn the screen and prove the pipeline works, with zero paperwork.
  2. Simulation — ZATCA's pre-production environment. From here on you use your company's real registered VAT/CR and address. This is the dress rehearsal before real tax invoices go out.
  3. Production — the live gateway. Real invoices, real tax liability, real government clearance.

01 / Finding the wizard

Where this screen lives

Log in → open Admin Settings → left menu group "Organization & Setup" → "ZATCA Phase 2 E-Invoicing"

Make sure the correct company is selected as your active company first (top of the app) — everything on this screen applies to whichever company you currently have active. If "ZATCA Phase 2 E-Invoicing" isn't in the menu at all, your account isn't a Company Admin or Super Admin for this company.

03 / The three environments at a glance

What's genuinely different between them

Testing

Sandbox

Uses ZATCA's own shared public test identity. Every Step 1 field pre-fills. OTP is always 123456. No real ZATCA portal account needed. Safe to redo as many times as you like.

Staging

Simulation

Your company's real VAT/CR/address, entered by you. Requires a real login to ZATCA's Fatoora Portal to fetch a one-time OTP. Mirrors production rules and validation, without real tax consequences.

Live Tax

Production

Same real-identity requirement as Simulation, but this is the official gateway — every invoice submitted here is a genuine legal filing with the Saudi tax authority.

Whichever one you're working on, the process is the identical 5-step sequence in §04 — only the answers you type in Step 1, and where the OTP in Step 3 comes from, differ.

05 / Sandbox specifics

One message in Step 5 that looks like an error but isn't

ZATCA's Sandbox is deliberately "static" — every company's sandbox onboarding gets issued the exact same test certificate, tied to ZATCA's own shared test identity, no matter what you typed in Step 1. This app detects that and automatically adjusts to make it work correctly.

Expected, not an error

"Production CSID activated for sandbox. ZATCA returned its known static test certificate, so this environment's signing key was switched to ZATCA's own matching sample private key — invoices will now sign correctly and validate cleanly."

This is the app correctly handling a documented ZATCA sandbox quirk, not a fault. Onboarding is genuinely complete. This adjustment applies to Sandbox only and never touches Simulation or Production.

06 / Simulation & Production specifics

The one step that happens outside this app

Steps 1, 2, 4, and 5 all happen entirely inside this app. Step 3's OTP is the one exception — for Simulation and Production, that code has to come from a real login to ZATCA's own Fatoora Portal, because it's ZATCA confirming you actually control the real company identity you entered in Step 1.

  1. Log in to ZATCA's Fatoora Portal using your company's real ZATCA/government credentials (outside this app).
  2. Find the option to onboard a new e-invoicing solution / generate an onboarding OTP for the environment you're working on. ZATCA's own portal screens are the authority on exact wording — they can change independently of this app.
  3. Copy the 6-digit code shown. Valid for roughly one hour, single-use.
  4. Immediately switch back to this app's Step 3, paste the code, and press Issue Compliance CSID right away.
A subtlety worth knowing before you start Step 2

The CSR generated in Step 2 only stays available in your browser for the current session. If you refresh the page or come back later, Step 3 will refuse to submit and tell you to regenerate it — this protects a real, single-use OTP from being wasted on a stale request. Do Step 2 and Step 3 back-to-back in one sitting.

Everything else genuinely, cryptographically belongs to your company for these two environments — there is no shared/static identity trick here the way there is for Sandbox; a real mismatch is a real problem and the app refuses to silently proceed.

07 / Going live: the active environment

Onboarded is not the same as "in use"

Completing the 5 steps for an environment does not automatically start sending real invoices there. A separate switch controls which single environment new invoices actually go to. Once an environment shows Onboarded, a "Set active" link appears on its card.

Guard rail — read this either way

You cannot set an environment active until it shows Onboarded — the app rejects the attempt with a clear message. There is no way to accidentally send real invoices to an environment that hasn't finished the 5 steps.

The one gotcha that can make invoices silently do nothing

A separate, company-wide "ZATCA Integration" switch (Admin Settings → Companies Directory, Super Admin only) turns on automatically the moment a company finishes onboarding Sandbox for the first time. If a company's first completed environment is Simulation or Production instead, this switch is never auto-enabled — every invoice sits at DISABLED with zero calls attempted, even though onboarding looks complete. If that's your situation, ask a Super Admin to check this toggle before concluding something is broken.

08 / Proving it worked

The actual pass/fail test

Onboarding is only real proof once a genuine invoice clears through it.

  1. Make sure the customer has the right details on file: a B2B customer needs a full ZATCA identity — VAT, building number, street, district, city, postal code. A B2C customer only needs a name.
  2. Create a normal invoice for that customer as you always would (Invoices or POS).
  3. Saving finishes instantly on screen — ZATCA submission happens moments later in the background. Reopen the invoice after a few seconds to see the result.
  4. Read the ZATCA status:
    • Cleared (B2B) or Reported (B2C) — success.
    • Rejected — open the ZATCA detail panel for the specific reason and correct it.
    • Not Submitted — see §07's crit callout; the company-wide switch is very likely off.

09 / Troubleshooting

Messages you might see, and exactly what they mean

"VAT/TIN must be exactly 15 digits, starting and ending with 3."
Step 1 rejection. Check the last digit, not just the first.
"No CSR is loaded in this session. Go back to Step 2..."
You reached Step 3 after refreshing or returning later. Go back to Step 2, generate again, then continue straight through.
"[ENVIRONMENT] already has an issued CSID..."
A confirmation dialog, not an error. Only proceed if you want to redo Step 3 onward.
"ZATCA issued a certificate that does not match this environment's private key."
On Simulation/Production, a real problem the app caught and refused to accept — nothing was saved. Retry; if it repeats, it needs investigation by whoever maintains the app.
"[ENVIRONMENT] has not completed onboarding yet — cannot set it as active."
Finish all 5 steps for that environment first.
An invoice sits at "Not Submitted" indefinitely.
Almost always the company-wide ZATCA Integration switch (§07). Have a Super Admin check it.
A B2B customer's invoice never attempts ZATCA submission.
The customer record is missing a required field. Complete the ZATCA identity fields on the customer, then resend.

Appendix — access & data, in plain terms

  • Only Company Admins and Super Admins can view or edit ZATCA onboarding for a company.
  • Sandbox, Simulation, and Production each store a completely separate identity, private key, and certificate.
  • The OTP typed in Step 3 is used once, immediately, and is never saved anywhere by the app.
  • Every company's ZATCA data is fully isolated from every other company on the Warraq platform.
