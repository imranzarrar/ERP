## 1. Purpose

Replace document-derived bookkeeping with a real double-entry ledger that sales, purchasing, inventory and (later) payroll all settle into, so that accounts receivable, accounts payable, bank, tax and every financial statement are queries over the same posted lines. This document states, once and traceably, **what the business needs**; the SRS states what the system shall do about it.

## 2. Business context and problem

Before 2.0 the system computed every report from documents (invoices, expenses, bills, vouchers). That is correct for a single entity but gives accountants no chart of accounts of their own, no manual adjustments, no analytical dimensions, no subledger tied to a control account, no multi-document receipts, no bank reconciliation and no period control beyond month close. Tier-1 ERP practice (SAP, Oracle, Dynamics) treats all of these as basics.

## 3. Objectives

1. One ledger, one posting engine, one report engine: no figure is calculated in two places.
2. The accountant can see, adjust, analyse and close the books without developer help.
3. Receivables and payables behave as subledgers: every party balance ties to its control account to the cent.
4. Money movements (receipts, payments, bank charges) are controlled, auditable and reconcilable to the bank.
5. Nothing can be posted outside the period rules, and every override is attributable.
6. The solution serves thousands of concurrent users across many companies, safely isolated.

### COA — Chart of accounts and account mapping

**BR-COA-01 Company-owned hierarchical chart** — Must, Built, Blueprint FR-COA-01, source Blueprint, sprint S1.
Every company has its own chart of accounts: code, English and Arabic name, type (asset, liability, equity, revenue, expense), parent heading and active flag. Headings group and total but take no postings.

**BR-COA-02 Starter chart at provisioning** — Must, Built, Blueprint FR-COA-03, source Blueprint, sprint S1.
A new company receives a standard Saudi chart automatically, with every posting role (receivable, payable, inventory, VAT, bank, revenue, cost of sales ...) mapped to exactly one account and its own account for each bank.

**BR-COA-03 Control accounts** — Must, Built, Blueprint FR-COA-02, source Blueprint, sprint S1.
Accounts for customers, vendors, stock, tax and banks are control accounts: only the document or subledger that owns them posts there, and manual journals refuse them.

**BR-COA-04 Retire, never delete** — Must, Built, Blueprint FR-COA-04, source Blueprint, sprint S1.
An account with postings can never be deleted. It can be deactivated only when its balance is zero and nothing depends on it; its type, role and bank link cannot change once used.

**BR-COA-05 Show dependants before deactivation** — Should, Partial, Blueprint FR-COA-05, source Blueprint, sprint S20.
Before an account is deactivated the user sees every configuration record that points at it (role mappings, banks, categories, customer and vendor profiles).

**BR-COA-06 Account mapping** — Must, Built, source Decision, sprint S1.
The accountant chooses which account each posting role and each expense category posts to. A change applies to future postings only; old lines keep the account they were posted to.

**BR-COA-07 Chart import and export** — Should, Built, source Decision, sprint S1.
The chart can be exported to Excel and imported from Excel with a preview; only the rows the user selects are saved and an existing account type is never changed.

**BR-COA-08 Audit of chart changes** — Must, Built, source Standard, sprint S1.
Creating, changing, importing, mapping and deactivating accounts is written to the audit trail.

### JNL — Journals and opening balances

**BR-JNL-01 Manual journals with maker-checker** — Must, Built, Blueprint FR-JE-01, source Decision, sprint S1, S11.
An accountant prepares a journal as a draft, submits it, and a different person with the posting right approves it (which posts it) or rejects it with a written reason, returning it to draft.

**BR-JNL-02 Balanced entries only** — Must, Built, Blueprint FR-JE-01, source Blueprint, sprint S1.
A journal entry whose debits do not equal its credits is refused by the server inside the same transaction as the write, for documents and for manual journals alike.

**BR-JNL-03 Posted entries are immutable** — Must, Built, Blueprint FR-JE-02, source Blueprint, sprint S1.
A posted entry is never updated or deleted. A correction is a reversing entry plus a new correct entry; a reversal can be made only once.

**BR-JNL-04 Source and dimension recorded** — Must, Built, Blueprint FR-JE-03, source Blueprint, sprint S1.
Every entry records its source (document type and id) and every line carries the dimension tags and party its account requires.

**BR-JNL-05 Entry numbering** — Must, Built, Blueprint FR-JE-04, source Blueprint, sprint S1.
Manual journals receive a unique sequential number per company; a discarded draft keeps its number on record.

**BR-JNL-06 Reversal of a manual journal** — Must, Built, source Decision, sprint S1.
A posted manual journal is reversed by a reversing draft that follows the same approval path; the original stays posted until the reversal is approved. Entries made by documents are reversed through their document, never by hand.

**BR-JNL-07 Self-approval safeguard** — Should, Built, Blueprint FR-APR-03, source Decision, sprint S1, S11.
A preparer cannot approve their own journal unless the company owner switches that safeguard on; when on, such journals are marked and the change is audited.

**BR-JNL-08 Journal listing and entry detail** — Must, Built, source Decision, sprint S1.
Every posting of the company, from documents and from manual journals, can be listed and filtered (period, status, source, text) and opened to its lines and its source document.

**BR-JNL-09 Opening balances** — Must, Built, source Decision, sprint S1.
A company that starts using the ledger part-way can post an opening entry: previewed first, balanced (a difference may be put to an equity account), posted once, with a confirmation, and reversible with a reason.

**BR-JNL-10 Amount-threshold approval rules** — Should, Partial, Blueprint FR-APR-01, FR-APR-02, FR-APR-03, FR-APR-04, FR-APR-05, FR-APR-06, source Blueprint, sprint S11, S11.
Approval rules per document type (journal, vendor bill, vendor payment, credit note) by amount, with several sequential levels, server-enforced, with no self-approval at any level and a full approval history; a document without a matching rule posts directly. Delivered so far (Sprint 11): vendor payments, vendor bills and credit notes, recall by the submitter, delegation with validity dates, the manager hierarchy, and the "paid at delivery" goods-receipt shortcut cannot bypass a rule. Manual journals follow a rule too when one matches their amount; with no matching rule they keep the single maker-checker step.

### PER — Periods, posting control and close

**BR-PER-01 Open fiscal month rule** — Must, Built, source Standard, sprint S5.
A dated posting must fall in an existing, open fiscal month and not in the future.

**BR-PER-02 VAT quarter freeze** — Must, Built, source Compliance, sprint S5.
A tax document may not be dated in a VAT quarter whose return is generated or filed; any other posting may not be dated in a filed quarter.

**BR-PER-03 Lock date** — Must, Built, source Standard, sprint S5.
The company sets a lock date; nothing may be dated on or before it except by a user holding the override right.

**BR-PER-04 Back-date window** — Must, Built, source Standard, sprint S5.
The company sets how many days back a posting may be dated; going further needs the back-date right.

**BR-PER-05 Reason for back-dating** — Must, Built, source Standard, sprint S5.
A posting older than a set number of days must carry a reason; a posting that used an override or a reason is written to the audit log.

**BR-PER-06 One guard for every dated posting** — Must, Built, source Platform, sprint S5.
Every route that posts something dated, or reverses something at its own date, passes through one posting guard, so no path can bypass the period rules. A manual journal is checked at submit and again at approval.

**BR-PER-07 Month close from posted lines** — Should, Partial, Blueprint FR-CLOSE-01, FR-CLOSE-02, source Blueprint, sprint S12.
Closing a fiscal month verifies the month trial balance from posted journal lines, locks the period and never zeroes revenue or expense accounts; the cached figure is a performance aid, never the only source.

**BR-PER-08 Month-close readiness checklist** — Should, Planned, Blueprint FR-CLOSE-03, source Blueprint, sprint S12.
Before a month closes the system shows one checklist: bank reconciliations completed, VAT quarters filed, no draft or pending documents dated in the month, and the other period prerequisites.

**BR-PER-09 Restricted month and adjustment period** — Should, Planned, Blueprint FR-CLOSE-07, FR-CLOSE-08, source Blueprint, sprint S12.
A month status between open and closed that only named users may post into, and a restricted adjustment period after year-end for controller / auditor correcting entries; a closed year is never reopened.

**BR-PER-10 Year-end close** — Must, Planned, Blueprint FR-CLOSE-04, FR-CLOSE-05, FR-CLOSE-06, source Blueprint, sprint S13.
Year-end close computes the year result from posted lines and posts exactly one closing entry into retained earnings, then opens the next year automatically; balance sheet accounts carry forward with no posting.

**BR-PER-11 Per-module close** — Could, Planned, source Standard, sprint S12.
Receivables, payables, inventory and the general ledger can each be closed on their own date, as in tier-1 systems.

### SAL — Sales documents (quotations, invoices, credit notes)

**BR-SAL-01 Quotations convert to invoices** — Must, Built, source Platform, sprint SB1.
A quotation can be created, edited, cancelled and converted into a tax invoice. A converted quotation is final. The conversion is an ordinary invoice and follows every invoice rule (numbering, period locks, e-invoicing).

**BR-SAL-02 Invoice payments, in part or in full** — Must, Built, source Platform, sprint SB1.
An invoice can be paid in several payments. Each payment is its own receipt voucher. A payment is limited to the remaining balance; a zero or negative amount, a cancelled invoice, an already paid invoice and a credit note cannot be paid. Only a user with the create right can record a payment, and only in the invoice's own company.

**BR-SAL-03 Cancelling an invoice** — Must, Built, source Platform, sprint SB1.
An invoice can be cancelled in an open period; the receipt voucher of a paid invoice is reversed by a reversal voucher, never deleted. An invoice dated in a filed VAT quarter cannot be cancelled; a ZATCA-cleared invoice cannot be cancelled (see BR-ZAT-02).

**BR-SAL-04 Credit notes** — Must, Built, source Platform, sprint SB1.
A credit note reverses an invoice. Only one credit note can be raised against an invoice. When the original was paid, the credit note reverses its receipt; when unpaid, no voucher is touched. Credit notes follow the approval rules when one matches.

**BR-SAL-05 Sales associate on an invoice** — Should, Built, source Platform, sprint SB1.
An invoice can name the employee who sold it. The employee must belong to the same company and be active; the field is optional.

### EXP — Expenses and recurring postings

**BR-EXP-01 Expense payments, in part or in full** — Must, Built, source Platform, sprint SB1.
An expense can be paid in several payments, each its own payment voucher, never above the remaining balance. Paid or cancelled expenses cannot be paid, and another company's expense is never found.

**BR-EXP-02 Cancelling an expense** — Must, Built, source Platform, sprint SB1.
An expense can be cancelled once, by a user with the cancel right. A paid expense is cancelled with a reversal voucher. An expense dated in a filed VAT quarter cannot be cancelled.

**BR-EXP-03 Recurring templates and accruals** — Should, Built, source Platform, sprint SB1.
A company keeps recurring expense templates (create, edit, switch off) and accrual entries (edit, cancel). Amounts must be valid; nothing is hard-deleted: a template is deactivated and an accrual cancelled, together with its linked recurring posting.

### PUR — Procurement and inventory

**BR-PUR-01 Purchase bills from goods receipts** — Must, Built, source Platform, sprint SB1.
A purchase bill is raised from goods receipt notes of one vendor. Its totals are computed on the server from the receipts, a receipt can be billed only once, a reversed receipt cannot be billed, and only a user with the create right can bill. A bill that matches an approval rule waits for approval.

**BR-PUR-02 Purchase bill payment and cancellation** — Must, Built, source Platform, sprint SB1.
A bill can be paid in part and then in full, never above the balance. An unpaid bill can be cancelled, which releases its receipts for re-billing; a bill with a payment cannot be cancelled. A bill dated in a filed VAT quarter cannot be cancelled.

**BR-PUR-03 Purchase returns** — Must, Built, source Platform, sprint SB1.
Goods can be returned to a vendor against a receipt: never more than was received or than remains after earlier returns, never against a reversed receipt. A return reduces stock and can be cancelled once (restoring stock). A return cannot be created while the current VAT quarter is filed.

**BR-PUR-04 Physical stock takes** — Must, Built, source Platform, sprint SB1.
A stock take snapshots the system quantity on the server and computes the variance. Finalising posts the counted quantity as the stock level, once. A draft can be cancelled; a completed one cannot.

**BR-PUR-05 Warehouse transfers** — Must, Built, source Platform, sprint SB1.
Stock moves between warehouses by a dispatch and a receiving. Dispatch deducts the source, receiving adds the quantity actually received, once even under concurrent requests. A dispatch still in transit can be cancelled to restore stock. Branch-restricted users act only on their own warehouses, and cross-company warehouses are refused.

### POS — Point of sale

**BR-POS-01 Cashier shifts** — Must, Built, source Platform, sprint SB1.
A cashier opens and closes a shift, recording actual and expected cash. Shifts are listed and closed only within their own company; opening and closing need the shift right and using the terminal needs the access right.

**BR-POS-02 Held sales** — Should, Built, source Platform, sprint SB1.
A sale in progress can be held and later resumed. Held sales are visible only within their company and need the terminal access right.

**BR-POS-03 POS sales are invoices** — Must, Built, source Compliance, sprint SB1.
A POS sale is an ordinary tax invoice and goes through the same e-invoicing pipeline; a POS cancel needs its own permission.

### ZAT — ZATCA e-invoicing

**BR-ZAT-01 E-invoicing is a per-company switch** — Must, Built, source Compliance, sprint SB1.
E-invoicing is off for a company until its onboarding enables it. With it off, an invoice lands as DISABLED with a real basic QR code and never touches the ZATCA gateway or the hash chain; with it on, the same invoice proceeds.

**BR-ZAT-02 Cancellation of e-invoiced documents** — Must, Built, source Compliance, sprint SB1.
An invoice that reached ZATCA (cleared or reported) or is being submitted cannot be cancelled; a credit note is the only reversal. An invoice that never reached ZATCA can be cancelled.

**BR-ZAT-03 Hash-chain integrity** — Must, Built, source Compliance, sprint SB1.
Every document of a company and environment sits on one hash chain that starts at genesis for each new company and environment. Resubmitting the tip reuses its position; resubmitting an earlier document after a later one mints a fresh position; cancelling the tip that never reached ZATCA gives its position back; a cleared document cannot be resubmitted; the issue time is locked once set.

**BR-ZAT-04 Safe storage of keys and signed documents** — Must, Built, source Compliance, sprint SB1.
ZATCA private keys are encrypted at rest (authenticated encryption, random IV, tamper detection, clear error when the secret is missing or wrong). The signed XML is stored compressed, losslessly, and is served on demand, never in list payloads, and never to another tenant.

### VAT — VAT returns and period locks

**BR-VAT-01 VAT return figures** — Must, Built, source Compliance, sprint SB1.
A quarterly VAT return is generated from the documents: sales, output VAT, purchases and input VAT, with credit notes netted. The return and the VAT summary report show the same numbers. A generated return can be deleted and regenerated, and a duplicate for the same quarter is refused.

**BR-VAT-02 Filing rules** — Must, Built, source Compliance, sprint SB1.
A return cannot be generated or filed while an invoice in its quarter has not completed e-invoicing. Quarters must be filed in order; a filed return is permanently locked.

**BR-VAT-03 A filed quarter locks its documents** — Must, Built, source Compliance, sprint SB1.
Once a quarter is filed (and while a generated return freezes it), no invoice, credit note, expense or purchase bill can be created in it, and none can be cancelled or converted into it. Quotations, requisitions, orders and stock takes are not locked.

**BR-VAT-04 VAT return rights and isolation** — Must, Built, source Compliance, sprint SB1.
Reading, generating, filing and deleting a return are separate rights, and a company can never see or touch another company's returns.

### MST — Master data, numbering and imports

**BR-MST-01 Document numbering** — Must, Built, source Platform, sprint SB1.
Every document type has its own numbering in the PREFIX-1001 style, issued sequentially with no duplicates or gaps even under concurrent requests, per document period, continuing from the legacy counter. Changing the policy needs the right and cannot change e-invoicing settings.

**BR-MST-02 Customer and vendor import** — Should, Built, source Platform, sprint SB1.
Customers and vendors can be loaded from a spreadsheet: a template, validation before saving (a business customer needs a valid VAT number and full address), update of an existing record matched by VAT number or phone, conflict detection, a row cap and the create right.

**BR-MST-03 Product import** — Should, Built, source Platform, sprint SB1.
Products can be loaded from a spreadsheet: template, validation without writing, categories resolved by name in the company, real SKUs assigned, an existing barcode updates instead of duplicating, a row cap and the create right.

**BR-MST-04 Master data rights and retirement** — Must, Built, source Platform, sprint SB1.
Master data uses create, read, update and delete rights, where delete means making a record inactive. Create does not allow editing an existing row and update does not allow creating one; an inactive record cannot be edited. Cancel sets a flag without changing the status and works once.

### ORG — Tenancy, users and company administration

**BR-ORG-01 Every request belongs to one company** — Must, Built, source Platform, sprint SB1.
Data returned and written is always for the company of the request: a user sees their own company; only a super-administrator can switch company, and the choice persists in the session. A company can never overwrite another company's customer, invoice, quotation or expense, and reports and dashboards are computed per company.

**BR-ORG-02 User accounts** — Must, Built, source Platform, sprint SB1.
Administrators can deactivate and reactivate users of their own company (never their own session), assign roles of the same company only, and a delegated user creating accounts can only create ordinary staff. Passwords need at least six characters with a letter and a digit.

**BR-ORG-03 Employees and job titles** — Should, Built, source Platform, sprint SB1.
Employees have a job title, an employee number issued in sequence (width and start configurable) and an optional branch. Duplicate active titles and numbers are refused, a deactivated title cannot be used, and a new login account must link to an active employee once the company has employees.

**BR-ORG-04 Sessions and sign-in** — Must, Built, source Platform, sprint SB1.
A session records the user and company at sign-in, expires, and is extended once past half of its lifetime. Only a super-administrator can list sessions across companies.

**BR-ORG-05 No bulk save of documents** — Must, Built, source Platform, sprint SB1.
There is no endpoint that overwrites posted documents in bulk. A document is written only through its own route, and a request naming an existing id cannot take over another document's lines.

**BR-ORG-06 Capital contributions** — Should, Built, source Platform, sprint SB1.
An investor's capital contribution can be recorded: it creates a receipt voucher and adds to capital contributed, accumulating across contributions. Amounts must be positive, banks active and investors in the company.

### REP — Reports and dashboard (documents)

**BR-REP-01 Server-side, paged and exact reports** — Must, Built, source Platform, sprint SB1.
Every register, statement and report is computed on the server for the active company. Long lists are paged in the database with the full count and totals over every row, with a no-paging path for print and export. An end date includes its whole last day, an invalid date is refused, a branch-restricted user never sees other branches, and a user without the report right is refused.

**BR-REP-02 Reports agree with the documents and with each other** — Must, Built, source Platform, sprint SB1.
Profit and loss, cash flow, balance sheet, trial balance, VAT summary, KPI cards, outstanding balances, the dashboard, the stock register and the ledger itself all reconcile to the source documents on a real book of invoices, credit notes, expenses and bills. Refunded money is not counted as collected and credited invoices are not pending.

### CFG — Document templates and translations

**BR-CFG-01 Print templates and translations** — Should, Built, source Platform, sprint SB1.
Each company has its own print templates (create, edit options and layout, activate one per language, delete); only an administrator manages them and no template can be changed across companies. Translations are one shared table managed only by a super-administrator, per key.

## 7. Constraints and assumptions

* Single company currency (SAR); multi-currency and intercompany are out of scope.
* Live (1.0) is changed by fixes only; all development is on the 2.0 track and reaches Live only by an explicit promotion (BR-NFR-06).
* Existing document-based reports remain the figures of record until the cut-over (BR-RPT-12).
* ZATCA e-invoicing code is not altered by this programme.
* Document dates, posting guard and permission model are platform standards that every later feature must follow.

## 8. Success criteria

* Ledger-vs-Documents agrees to the cent for every company; the consistency check shows no error-level failure.
* Every built requirement has automated test coverage traced in the matrix (08).
* A new company can complete the user-manual flow (06) without developer help.
