Storefront — built for retail volume, not a machine shop

Fair note on the first pass: warm graphite and burnt copper read as heavy industrial, dull for a product mostly serving retailers. This revision keeps the same structure but replaces the palette entirely — crisp near-white surfaces instead of muddy warm gray, and a single confident, saturated accent (a warm red-orange, the color of a sale tag or a receipt stamp) instead of a desaturated metal tone. Semantic status colors are also brightened to standard, punchy tones — the meanings (green/amber/red/blue) are unchanged from StatusPill.tsx, just no longer muted.
