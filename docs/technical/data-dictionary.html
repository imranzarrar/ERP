company_accounts

A company's own chart of accounts (General Ledger Step 1). Every company gets a standard Saudi starter chart (server/lib/chartOfAccounts.ts) that it may rename, recode and extend. systemKey ties an account to one of the fixed posting roles above (AR, AP, VAT output...): the document routes keep posting by systemKey, and this table is what makes that key mean "this company's account 1100" without changing a single existing journal line. Foreign keys that carry the company (parent, journal lines, manual journal lines) are COMPOSITE (id, company_id), so the database itself refuses a reference to another company's account, on top of the row-level-security policy and the route's own ownership checks.

system_accounts

The fixed, canonical set of accounts every ledger posting refers to by key — see docs/procurement-ledger-review (Phase 1 design) for the full rationale. Genuinely cross-tenant by design (no companyId column, same convention as translations above): every company reads the same fixed account list today. When a real company-configurable chart of accounts ships, a separate companyAccountMappings(companyId, systemAccountKey, realAccountCode, realAccountName) table attaches to these same keys without a schema change here.

journal_entries

Journal entry header — one row per posted transaction. Every report that needs a real, reconciling account balance (AR, AP, VAT, COGS, ...) reads from journalLines below, never straight from invoices/expenses/purchaseBills, the same way vouchers already exist for cash movement but nothing else. See the Phase 1 design doc for the full posting-rules table (currently 19 transaction types) this implements.

posting_policies

Posting control (docs/design-notes/posting-control.md): one row per company that wants rules on top of the open / closed fiscal month. A company with no row has none of them. lock_date: nothing may be dated on or before it (except for the right postingControl.overrideLock); max_back_days: nothing may be dated more than that many days before today (except for postingControl.backDate); reason_after_days: a posting older than that needs a written reason.

payment_allocations

Payment allocation (Blueprint FR-AR-02 / FR-AP-04): which documents a receipt or payment settles, and for how much. A multi-document receipt (CustomerReceipt) or payment (VendorPayment) is one voucher + one journal entry (the party's control account against the bank) + these rows; what is not allocated stays on account with the party. An allocation is never edited: cancelling the receipt marks its rows reversed and gives the money back to the documents.

bank_reconciliations

Bank reconciliation (Blueprint FR-BANK-01..05). One session per bank account per statement period: the statement's ending balance and date are entered, every posted ledger line of the bank's account up to that date is listed, and each is marked cleared (matched to a statement line, or ticked). It can be completed only when the cleared balance equals the statement balance and every statement line is matched; a completed session locks its lines. A bank fee / interest / timing error is fixed by a journal entry posted from inside the session.
